# Marketing strategy

Owned by Selly. See `docs/strategy/README.md` for sequencing — this
strategy assumes `docs/strategy/beta-testing-strategy.md`'s Phase 1 has
validated the redesign and `docs/strategy/subscription-strategy.md`'s
tiers exist before any spend/effort goes toward driving paid conversion
specifically (driving traffic to a free product with no monetization
path yet is fine and should start earlier — see §5's phasing).

**Grounding:** `docs/design-guidelines.md`'s positioning (classy,
luxurious, investor-focused, explicitly *not* the "dense grid, VIP/HOT
badges, urgency-toned copy" style of typical Bulgarian portals) and
`docs/property-filter-spec.md`'s real feature set drive every channel
recommendation below — this is not generic growth-marketing boilerplate.

---

## 1. Positioning and audience (from the existing docs, not invented)

**Audience:** Bulgarian property *investors* (per the repo's own framing
and `docs/design-guidelines.md`'s luxury-adjacent, "deal-sourcing tool"
positioning) — not casual home-buyers. This matters for channel choice:
investors cluster in identifiable online communities and respond to
data/analysis-driven content, not lifestyle imagery alone.

**Positioning statement (Recommendation — needs Kiril's sign-off):**
*"The only place that shows you every Bulgarian property listing across
all major portals in one place, with the investor signals (motivation
score, price history) the portals themselves don't show you."* This
leads with the two genuinely differentiating, already-built capabilities
(`docs/backlog.md` item 7's motivation score, and the multi-portal
aggregation Nosy's spec calls "arguably the single highest-value feature
to prioritize") rather than generic "browse listings" language every
competing portal already uses. (An earlier draft of this tagline also
listed "comparables" alongside motivation score and price history —
dropped here since Comparables is `docs/backlog.md` item 10, unshipped,
and this paragraph's own framing is explicitly "already-built
capabilities," not a forward-looking one. Add it back once item 10
ships, not before.)

## 2. Primary channel: programmatic SEO (the highest-leverage automated
channel available, and it's nearly free to run)

This is the single best-fit channel for this specific product, for a
reason worth stating plainly: **imotenradar already has the raw data to
generate genuinely useful, unique content at scale, automatically,
because the scrapers already collect it.**

- **What to generate — launch scope, built on what's shipped now, not a
  future item:** area/city investment-guide pages, one per city/oblast,
  built entirely from aggregates that already exist today, independent
  of any unshipped backlog item: the per-`(city, area)` average price/m²
  (`area_avg_price_per_sqm`) every scraper already computes for every
  listing and `sync_to_supabase.py` already writes through to
  `merged_listings` (see its `SOURCE_FIELDS`/`MERGED_FIELDS`), plus the
  motivation-score work (`docs/backlog.md` item 7, DONE) that already
  flags how many active listings in an area sit meaningfully below that
  average ("Hot"/"Warm"). A first-launch page needs nothing more than:
  "Property investment guide: [Area] — average price/m², X active
  listings across 8 portals, Y currently priced below the area average."
  This is deliberately **not** the same thing as the fuller Market Data
  hub / Comparables aggregation work in `docs/backlog.md` item 11
  (Postcode/city Performance, Adverts Evolution, a real historical
  price-trend chart, days-on-market trends) — item 11 is sequenced near
  the bottom of the backlog, after items 8/9/10, well after
  `docs/strategy/launch-strategy.md`'s own Stage 0→1 gate (items 5/8/9)
  and well before it would realistically ship, while this SEO channel is
  meant to go live *during* Stage 1 (`docs/strategy/launch-strategy.md`
  §2). Scoping the launch pages to what item 7 and the existing
  per-area averages already compute means the SEO engine doesn't wait on
  item 11 at all.
- **Later enhancement, not a blocking dependency:** once `docs/backlog.md`
  item 11 ships, the same already-live, already-indexed pages gain
  days-on-market trends, a real historical price-trend chart, and
  Adverts Evolution stock-change data — an enrichment of pages that
  already exist, not a precondition for launching them.
- **Why this works especially well here:** most Bulgarian property
  portals (per `docs/design-guidelines.md`'s characterization of the
  genre) do not publish this kind of aggregate market-analysis content —
  it's a genuine content gap, not a crowded keyword space, and it plays
  directly to data imotenradar already has and competitors would have to
  build fresh.
- **Automation mechanism:** these pages should be **generated by a
  script** (templated HTML/markdown populated from the same `data/*.json`
  files the scrapers already produce, regenerated on the same cadence as
  the scrapers themselves — daily/every-6-hours per the existing GitHub
  Actions cadence) rather than hand-written — this is the single
  highest-leverage "AI/automation running it, not manual input" move
  available in the entire marketing plan, since it produces hundreds of
  indexable pages from zero ongoing manual effort once built.
- **What still needs a human, and why:** the initial page **template**
  and **on-page copy structure** need one round of human/AI-assisted
  writing and design review before the generator is trusted to run
  unattended at scale (to confirm it reads as "classy/luxurious" per
  the design guidelines, not as thin programmatic-SEO spam, which is a
  real reputational and search-quality risk if done carelessly). This is
  a one-time setup cost, not a recurring one — flagged narrowly rather
  than treated as ongoing manual work.
- **Build note:** this crosses into implementation (a page generator
  script) — flagged here as the marketing rationale/spec for whoever on
  the engineering side (Bossy/Dessy) picks it up; Selly doesn't build it
  herself.

## 3. Secondary automated channel: email

**Recommendation — needs Kiril's sign-off** on tooling: use an ESP with
real automation/drip capability at low cost for early volume — **Brevo**
(generous free tier, EU-based/GDPR-friendly, good fit for a Bulgarian/EU
audience) over Mailchimp (pricier at this scale) as the lead
recommendation.

- **Trigger-based drip sequences, not manual sends:**
  - New free-tier signup (once accounts exist for paid tiers per
    `docs/strategy/subscription-strategy.md` — free tier itself has no
    signup today and shouldn't gain one just for marketing purposes, see
    §5) → a short onboarding sequence pointing at Lead Generators/saved
    searches, the core retention loop.
  - Lead Generator created with 0 saved listings after 3 days → a single
    nudge email pointing back at the feature (recoverable churn, cheap
    to automate).
  - Paid-tier trial/beta-discount user approaching renewal or trial end
    → automated reminder + value-recap email (driven by Stripe webhook
    data, same infrastructure `docs/strategy/subscription-strategy.md`
    §4 already specs).
  - **Recommendation — needs Kiril's sign-off, not yet scoped:**
    weekly/bi-weekly automated digest of new high-motivation-score
    listings matching a user's saved Lead Generators — the same "new
    since last check" mechanic `docs/property-filter-spec.md` section 1
    documents Property Filter using, adapted to email. This is a
    genuine product feature as much as a marketing one (re-engagement
    via real value, not a promotional blast), and its *content* would be
    generated from data the product already computes — the motivation
    score (`docs/backlog.md` item 7, DONE) and the same matching logic
    behind the in-app "new since last check" badge (item 9). But
    **delivering it as email is new, unscoped build work**, not
    something item 9 already covers: item 9 only builds the in-app badge
    (an in-app notification, no outbound email), and `docs/backlog.md`'s
    "Gaps in Nosy's spec" section explicitly lists "alert-email behavior
    (vs. in-app notifications)" as an open, unspecified gap needing its
    own follow-up capture, not confirmed scope. See
    `docs/strategy/subscription-strategy.md` §2, which flags this exact
    same gap for the same feature (a paid-tier digest) — both documents
    describe one shared, not-yet-scoped build item, not two independently
    scoped ones.
- **What still needs a human:** the initial drip sequence copy (written
  once, per sequence, not per-send) and periodic (quarterly, not
  per-email) review that automated copy still matches the brand voice —
  flagged narrowly, not an ongoing per-send task.

## 4. Referral mechanics (self-serve, automated)

**Recommendation — needs Kiril's sign-off:** a simple, self-serve
referral credit (e.g. "give a month, get a month" — both the referrer
and the referred new paid subscriber get one free month) implemented
entirely through Stripe (a unique referral link/code per paying
customer, applied automatically as a coupon at checkout, credit applied
automatically via Stripe's own coupon/credit system) — zero manual
tracking or manual credit-granting required. This only makes sense once
paid tiers exist (`docs/strategy/subscription-strategy.md`), so it's a
Phase-2-of-marketing item, not a launch-day one.

## 5. What's explicitly deferred, and why (scalability discipline)

**Not recommended for the automation-first launch phase:**

- **Social media content calendars** (Instagram/Facebook/LinkedIn
  posting) — flagged as the one broadly-used "automated marketing"
  channel Selly is **not** recommending prioritizing, and saying why
  plainly rather than defaulting to it: social content for a B2B-leaning
  investor tool has a weak fit for this audience (investors researching
  a market cluster in forums/communities and search, not social feeds),
  and even "scheduled" social content still needs an ongoing supply of
  genuinely new visual/written material (property photography rights are
  a real constraint too — the listing photos are scraped from third-party
  portals, not owned, so they can't be freely reused in promotional
  social content without checking usage rights portal-by-portal, a real
  open question flagged below). SEO content generation (§2) produces
  more indexable, compounding value per hour of setup than a social
  calendar would for this specific product and audience.
- **Paid ads** — premature before `docs/strategy/subscription-strategy.md`'s
  monetization exists to make paid acquisition math work, and before
  `docs/strategy/beta-testing-strategy.md`'s Phase 1 confirms the product
  itself retains users (spending to acquire users into a product that
  doesn't yet retain them is the classic mistake to avoid). Revisit once
  Phase 2 of the beta gives real conversion-rate data to model CAC
  against.
- **Manual community/forum engagement** — a real thing many early-stage
  products do (answering questions in property-investor forums, subtly
  mentioning the tool) but this is inherently manual, ongoing, and hard
  to automate well without reading as spam — flagged as explicitly out
  of scope for this AI-first plan rather than silently assumed. If Kiril
  wants a light version of this, it's a small, bounded weekly time cost,
  not a channel to build automation around.
