## New this session - single-snapshot price-parsing blips producing false price-drop badges (verified via internal consistency, not portal access)

Scanned all 8 portals' `history_*.json(.gz)` for consecutive-snapshot price ratios >3x or <0.33x: 75 total across ~417k listings. Of these, **18 have a very specific, verifiable signature**: exactly one snapshot sits at ~10x (or, in one case, ~100x) the price of BOTH its immediate neighbor before AND after, i.e. the sequence goes stable→spike/drop→back-to-stable, with the "before" and "after" values matching or nearly matching each other. This pattern repeats identically across 5 independently-coded scrapers (alo.bg, olx.bg, homes.bg, bazar.bg, imot.bg), which is itself evidence it's a parsing/data artifact rather than 18 unrelated real relistings that all happened to move by a suspiciously round 10x and then reverse.

This matters because `price_drop_count`/`drop_pct` are computed directly off these (corrupted) `price_history` entries and are currently live-wrong on real listings right now:
- **`imot_1c178582471897363`** (imot.bg, active) - stored `price_history`: 13,503,440 EUR (2026-08-25) → 134,500 EUR (2026-09-11, held through today). Current `price_drop_count: 1`, **`drop_pct: 99.0`** - the live site is showing a "99% price drop" badge on this listing right now. 134,500 EUR / 99 sqm = 1,359 EUR/sqm, which matches the listing's own `area_avg_price_per_sqm` (1,386 EUR/sqm, `pct_vs_area_avg: -1.9%`) almost exactly - strong internal evidence the real, stable price is 134,500 and the first snapshot (13.5M) was a corrupted read.
- `alo_11152598` (Ямбол): history 1,400,000 → 140,000, `drop_pct: 90.0` currently live.
- `alo_11394245` (Бургас): history 1,240,000 → 124,000, `drop_pct: 90.0` currently live.
- `olx_a37xJ`: history 1,450,000 → 145,000, `drop_pct: 90.0` currently live.
- `olx_a5vye` (Стара Загора/Мъглиж house, 100 sqm): history 480,000 → 48,000 → 48,000 (confirmed twice now). 48,000/100sqm = 480 EUR/sqm, realistic for a small-town house vs. 4,800 EUR/sqm being implausible for that location - `drop_pct: 90.0` is very likely a false badge.
- `alo_10264139`, `alo_11207214`, `alo_11011304`, `homes_36038`, `bazar_50846097`, `bazar_55990322`, `bazar_54808534`, `olx_a3AhK`, `olx_a3HSF` (partial) also carry the same isolated-10x-blip signature in their stored `price_history`, several with the same live-wrong `drop_pct`.

None of the 18 currently show a *live current price* that's itself the bad value (all have since re-scraped to the plausible figure) - the live-user-facing damage is specifically the false price-drop/increase badges and polluted price-history chart data, not the displayed price itself. Cannot tell from this sandbox (no portal access) whether this is a scraper-side digit-parsing bug or the source portals' own sellers briefly mistyping a price and then fixing it - either explanation is consistent with the data. The remaining 57 of the 75 total >3x/<0.33x anomalies were not individually verified this session (time/egress-limited) - not asserted as bugs, just not checked.
