## FINDING 1 - alo.bg: sqm silently dropped for ~12,400 active listings even though the portal's own scraped title states the size

Across all active (non-removed) listings in `data/leads_alo.json`, 12,410 of 77,769 have `sqm: null` while their own `title` field (scraped from alo.bg) plainly states a size in "кв.м." Every other portal's leads file (bazar, bcpea, homes, imot, imoti.bg, olx) has zero such cases - olx.bg titles carry the same "NN кв.м" pattern about as often as alo.bg's do (172/3000 vs 391/3000 sampled) and olx.bg's sqm field is populated correctly every time, so this isn't "some portals just don't expose size in title" - it's specific to alo.bg's own sqm extraction. 2,807 of the 12,410 have already been through the detail-page backfill pass (`detail_checked`/`_detail_fetched: true`) and still have `sqm: null`, ruling out "just not backfilled yet."

Concrete example - alo_11305291 (https://www.alo.bg/3-staen-pomorie-sveti-georgi-122-kv-m-244000-11305291):
- Stored: `"sqm": null, "price_per_sqm": null, "area_avg_price_per_sqm": null, "pct_vs_area_avg": null, "_detail_fetched": true`
- Same record's own `title`: "...3-стаен, Поморие, Свети Георги, 122 кв.м., 244000 €..." and `description`: "3-стаен, Поморие, Свети Георги, 122 кв.м., 244000 €" - both say 122 sqm, sourced from alo.bg itself.

More of the same shape: alo_11387869 ("...81 кв.м...", sqm null), alo_11340138 ("...73 кв.м...", sqm null), alo_11371272 ("...62 кв.м...", sqm null), alo_11339196 ("...30 кв.м...", sqm null), alo_11367144 ("...137 кв.м...", sqm null) - all `detail_checked`/`_detail_fetched: true`.

Impact: this isn't just a missing display field - `price_per_sqm`, `area_avg_price_per_sqm`, and `pct_vs_area_avg` are all null in lockstep on these records, so any sqm-based sort/filter and the comparables logic silently skip ~16% of alo.bg's active inventory.

## FINDING 2 - alo.bg (+ a smaller olx.bg echo): apartment listings misclassified as "business"/"land"/"garage" because of an agency marketing phrase, not the property itself

Root cause, confirmed by reading `category_classifier.py`: the classifier has a documented, deliberate fix (`_resolve_subject_over_amenity`, `AMENITY_CATEGORIES`/`SUBJECT_CATEGORIES`, lines ~400-461) that lets a SUBJECT keyword (flat/house/land) beat an AMENITY keyword (garage/shop/business) when the subject word appears *before* the amenity word in the title - because real listings are subject-first, amenity-appended ("Тристаен апартамент ... с гараж"). But a batch of alo.bg agency listings open with the marketing idiom "Ключ в офиса!" ("key is at our office," meaning "call the agency," nothing to do with the property) *before* stating the real subject, e.g. title "Ключ в офиса! Тристаен апартамент в кв. Сарафово с двор" - here "офис" (business keyword) sits to the left of "апартамент" (flat keyword), which is exactly backwards from what the existing positional fix expects, so business wins instead of flat.

Confirmed via the committed data (title text is the portal's own, no external fetch needed):
- 18 alo.bg listings with this exact "Ключ в офиса! + апартамент/-стаен" title shape are stored with `category: "business"` (all `category_confidence: "low"`), e.g. alo_10907533 (https://www.alo.bg/klyuch-v-ofisa-tristaen-apartament-v-kv-sarafovo-s-dvor-10907533, title "...Ключ в офиса! Тристаен апартамент в кв. Сарафово с двор..."), alo_11052533, alo_10703621, alo_11259634, alo_11230234, alo_10194069, alo_11178519, alo_11194583, alo_11209447, alo_11194248, alo_10873127, alo_11178525, alo_11037744, alo_11411314, and 4 more.
- Same idiom, same bug, cross-posted to olx.bg: olx_a5hm2 (https://www.olx.bg/d/ad/klyuch-v-ofisa-tristaen-apartament-v-kv-sarafovo-s-dvor-CID368-IDa5hm2.html), category "business".
- A related, separate false positive on alo.bg: "дворно място" ("with a yard") is one of `CATEGORY_KEYWORDS["land"]`'s phrases, so an apartment whose title mentions its own yard gets bucketed "land" instead of "flat" - e.g. alo_9896852 (title "...двустаен апартамент с дворно място, сграда на 100м от плажа..., Царево...", category "land") and alo_10965642 (title "Център! Многостаен! Дворно Място! Център, Търговище", category "land").

This is not a cosmetic tag: `sync_to_supabase.py`'s `type_filter_bucket()` reads `category` directly for every non-bcpea portal (`CATEGORY_TO_BUCKET.get(l.get("category"), "other")` - there's no `category_confidence` check anywhere in that function or in the frontend), so these genuine flats show up live under the Business/Land filter bucket instead of Flat, and are missing from the Flat bucket entirely.

## ONE THING I CHECKED AND RULED OUT (noting so it isn't re-flagged)

`data/leads_bcpea.json` stores `category: "apartment"` on 45 listings whose own title literally starts with "Гараж" (e.g. bcpea_90553, "Гараж, Варна"). This looks like the same bug shape at first glance, but it isn't live-impacting - `sync_to_supabase.py`'s `type_filter_bucket()` has an explicit bcpea-only branch (with its own comment acknowledging bcpea's raw `category` column is known-bad) that ignores that field entirely for this portal and instead calls `bcpea_type_match()` against the title's controlled vocabulary, which correctly resolves "Гараж, ..." to bucket "garage". So the live filter is correct; only the unused raw field in the committed JSON is wrong. Not reporting as a bug.
