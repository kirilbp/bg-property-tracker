## New finding today - `group_listings()` has no category gate at all; ~8% of cross-portal merges mix genuinely different property types

Ran the real, unmodified `group_listings()`/`load_all_listings()` pipeline from `sync_to_supabase.py` against today's full committed dataset (409,835 listings → 66,868 multi-member merged groups). Read the function end to end: neither the sqm-tolerant union pass nor the sqm-less attach pass ever compares `category` - only `city_key`, normalized `area`, price tolerance, and (when present) sqm tolerance. This is a real, distinct gap from the already-fixed same-portal mass-development issue (backlog item 60) and the (portal,url) conflict fix (item 64) - neither of those touches category.

Quantified: 5,332 of 66,868 multi-member groups (~8%) currently contain 2+ different `category` values. Breakdown of the largest combinations: flat+house 2,084, flat+land 1,788, flat+shop 514, flat+garage 418, business+flat 312, house+land 64 (a `('apartment','flat')` pairing, 30 groups, is a benign label-synonym artifact of `CATEGORY_TO_BUCKET` mapping older scrapers' "apartment" to newer scrapers' "flat" - not a real mismatch, excluded from the concerning count).

Two confirmed, spot-checked concrete examples (land parcel merged with a finished apartment purely on matching city+area+coincidental price, with sqm absent on the apartment side so the sqm gate never applies):
- `homes_199660` (https://www.homes.bg/offer/parcel-za-prodazhba/parcel-5001m2-burgas-gr.pomorie/lp199660, "Парцел, 5001m²", Бургас/гр.Поморие, category=land, 310,000 EUR) merged with `alo_11267004` (https://www.alo.bg/mnogostaen-apartament-s-akt-16-v-gr-pomorie-11267004, "Многостаен апартамент с АКТ 16", Бургас/Поморие, category=flat, sqm=None, 310,000 EUR) - a 5,001m² land plot and a finished multi-room apartment now show as "the same listing."
- `homes_24600` (https://www.homes.bg/offer/zemya-za-prodazhba/zemedelska-zemya-11250m2-plovdiv-zhk.-zaharna-fabrika/la24600, "Земеделска земя, 11250m²", Пловдив/жк. Захарна фабрика, category=land, 225,000 EUR) merged with `bazar_55736534` (https://bazar.bg/obiava-55736534/sobstvenik-prodava-mezonet-v-tsentara-na-gr-plovdiv, a maisonette apartment, Пловдив/Захарна фабрика, category=flat, sqm=None, 225,000 EUR) - an 11,250m² farmland plot merged with a residential maisonette.
