    What is this?

    BG Property Tracker scrapes apartment-for-sale listings across Sofia from 7 portals plus nationwide court-ordered property auctions, tracks each listing's price history over time, and surfaces the ones most likely to be a motivated-seller deal.

    Sources

    imoti.net, alo.bg, homes.bg, imot.bg, olx.bg, bazar.bg, imoti.bg, sales.bcpea.org. imoti.net and alo.bg refresh every 24 hours (they're large sites with 300+ pages each); the other 6 refresh every 6 hours. sales.bcpea.org lists public property auctions ("публични продани") run by court-appointed enforcement agents nationwide - its price is a legal minimum starting bid, not a negotiable asking price.

    Motivation score

    A 0–100 score across five signals, each capped from real distributions measured against the live tracked dataset, not guessed:

    • Relisted (up to 25 pts) — 10 points for having been delisted and relisted at all, plus up to 15 more if it came back cheaper (capped at a 15% price cut).
    • Distinct price reductions (up to 20 pts) — capped at 3 separate cuts, not just whether it was ever reduced.
    • Size of the price drop (up to 20 pts) — capped at a 20% total drop.
    • Days on market (up to 20 pts) — capped at 180 days. Most listings score low here right now simply because nationwide tracking is still young (most inventory has only been tracked a few weeks) - not because they're not stale.
    • Below the area average price/m² (up to 15 pts) — capped at 40% below average, and only counted when that figure isn't already flagged ⚠️ Unverified (a likely data error, not a real bargain).

    When a listing has no usable area-average figure (roughly 4 in 10 listings today, mostly on portals with sparser size data), the other four signals are rescaled to still reach 100 rather than capping that listing's ceiling for a data gap outside its control. We still can't tell a listing that sold and was relisted by a new owner apart from one that simply failed to sell and came back unchanged - relisting is a real, now-scored signal, but an ambiguous one, not a confirmed "failed sale" flag. Multi-portal listing (the same property posted on more than one site at once) isn't factored in yet. Treat the score as a strong first filter, not a verdict.

    Badges

    • 🔥 Hot deal (≥40) / Warm (≥15) — motivation score thresholds, recalibrated against the real achievable range now that days-on-market is still compressed (see above) - these will likely need revisiting again as tracking history matures.
    • X% below/above area avg — this listing's €/m² vs. the average for its area, across all sources.
    • ⚠️ Unverified price — the €/m² is far enough from the area average that it might be a data error (e.g. a misparsed price) rather than a real deal. Worth double-checking against the original listing.
    • ↓ X% since first seen — price has dropped since this listing was first scraped.

    Land registry

    The "Check land registry" link opens Bulgaria's official land registry portal. You'll need the exact address from the listing page, a free account, and pay a small fee per search — this app doesn't automate that lookup.

    Duplicate listings

    The same apartment is often cross-posted to several portals. Listings that match closely enough on price, size, and area are automatically combined into one card — the "+ portal" badges show where else it's listed, and its detail page has a small button per source to switch between each portal's own photos, price, and description. This is a best-effort match (no photo comparison, just price/size/area), so it can occasionally miss a real duplicate or, rarely, combine two coincidentally similar listings.
